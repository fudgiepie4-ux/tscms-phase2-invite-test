Faith & Family Song Submissions

For Red Dirt & Redemption — Sundays at 3 PM CT

Got a faith and family song the Sunday show should hear? Send it in as an MP3 and help fill the show with more music.

How it works

  1. Fill in the short form below and tick the agreement box.
  2. Tap SEND BY EMAIL. Your email app opens with a prepared message.
  3. Attach your MP3 file to that email, then press Send yourself.

⚠️ Before you tap Send, look for the paperclip 📎 on your email. If there's no paperclip, your song isn't attached yet. The website cannot attach the file for you — email apps don't allow that — so this step is yours.

MP3 files only. Email attachments work best below approximately 25 MB; limits vary by email app. Suno, YouTube, and Facebook links are not accepted as submissions — the show needs the actual MP3 file to play.

This website does not collect or store your submission. Your sent email is the submission record.

Prefer to skip the form? Just email your MP3 directly to reddirtredemption@yahoo.com with your name, your Facebook name, and the song title.

Short terms

1. What to send

Faith and family songs only, as an actual MP3 file attached to your email. Links do not substitute for files.

2. Rights

You keep ownership of your song. By sending it, you give the show non-exclusive, royalty-free permission to receive, review, store, and, if picked, play it on Red Dirt & Redemption. You confirm you own the song or have the right to share it.

3. Selection

Sending a song does not guarantee it will be played. The show team picks what fits each Sunday.

4. Stopping future plays

You may ask the show to stop future plays of your song by emailing reddirtredemption@yahoo.com. Past shows already aired can't be undone.

5. Privacy

The website itself does not store or send form entries. When you tap Send By Email, your email app opens a draft with your name, Facebook name, Suno name, song title, artist, notes, guest interest, and the agreement you checked. Your email provider processes the message and the show team reviews it in their inbox.
