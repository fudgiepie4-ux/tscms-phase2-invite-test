What the Drive-In is
The TSCMS Drive-In is our community music-video show. Members send in a music video, and when we’ve gathered about two hours’ worth of approved videos, we premiere them together on a Monday Music Video Night.
What to send
• A video FILE — MP4 or MOV. Send the actual file, attached to your email.
• No Suno links. No YouTube links. Links can’t be accepted — files only, please.
• File too big to attach? A Google Drive link to the actual video file counts too — we download it and check it's a real video file. (Suno and YouTube links still never count.)
• One song per video.
How to send it
• Email your video file to fudgiepie4@gmail.com
• Subject line: TSCMS Drive-In Submission — [your song title]
• If your email program lets you add a label, use Drive-In Submissions.
What happens after you send it
• Every video is reviewed before it joins the show.
• Videos wait in the pool until there’s about two hours of approved video — then a premiere night is announced.
• Sending a video doesn’t guarantee it will be shown, but every submission is looked at and appreciated.
What you’re agreeing to
By sending a video, you’re telling us:
• The video is yours to share (your song, your video, or you have permission to send it).
• TSCMS may play it publicly as part of the Drive-In show and station pages.
• You’re not asking for payment — the Drive-In is a free community showcase.
Questions
Write to us at fudgiepie4@gmail.com — a real person reads every message.
These terms may be updated. The version number and date at the top of this page always show the current terms.
